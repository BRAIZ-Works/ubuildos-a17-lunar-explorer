## Page 1 | A digital design. A physical boundary.

A17 Lunar Explorer is a complete digital engineering package for its released scope. The rocket has not been fabricated, physically validated, or flown.

## Page 2 | What is inside

Design: CAD and printable files, drawings, renders, and original graphics. Build reference: documentation, inspection and safety guidance, and a bill of materials. Models: OpenRocket artifacts and modeled trajectory outputs. Traceability: public claims, rights, and provenance records.

## Page 3 | The assembly exists as files

Image: digital render of a tall, gold three-fin model-rocket design. The CAD and simulation can be inspected before a physical article exists. A modeled result is predicted from the digital design; this release does not provide a measured real-world result.

## Page 4 | What is not claimed

Fabrication: not performed. Ground testing: no physical validation established. Flight: not performed. Performance: no measured or guaranteed result. No NASA affiliation, sponsorship, approval, endorsement, official status, or official-replica status.

## Page 5 | Ask the evidence the right question

The files show what was designed: geometry, part relationships, modeled results, and documented assumptions. Physical evidence would show fabrication quality, measured dimensions, ground tests, and flight behavior. This release keeps those states distinct.
