BRAIZ Works™ · A17 Lunar Explorer

A17 Lunar Explorer is a digital engineering release for an independent educational model-rocket project.

The released package contains documentation, CAD and printable files, drawings, a bill of materials, modeled simulation artifacts, original graphics, and provenance records.

Here is the evidence boundary:

- Digital engineering: complete for the released scope.
- Fabrication: not performed.
- Physical validation: not performed.
- Flight validation: not performed.
- Simulation: modeled, not measured flight data.

The files make the design inspectable. They do not establish a built or flown rocket, guaranteed performance, or NASA affiliation, approval, sponsorship, endorsement, or official-replica status.
