# Public Scope and Limitations

- Digital engineering scope: complete for the admitted source.
- Fabrication: not performed.
- Physical validation: not performed.
- Flight validation: not performed.
- Simulation: modeled only; not measured flight data.
- Performance: no guaranteed performance claim.
- NASA status: no affiliation, endorsement, approval, sponsorship, official status, or official-replica claim.
- Physical use: commercially certified hobby rocket motors only; no motor fabrication, propellant recipes, or motor modification are included or authorized.
- Before any physical launch, current site, jurisdiction, safety-code, motor-certification, and adult-supervision requirements must be checked.
