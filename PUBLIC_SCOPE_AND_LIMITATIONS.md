- A complete digital engineering package for its published scope: documentation, CAD/print files, drawings, bill of materials, modeled simulation artifacts, original graphics, and provenance records.

## Not established

The design and modeled values may need revision after measurement or testing. Any future physical evidence requires its own qualified update; it is not inferred from the present files.

## Safety boundary

This digital release does not authorize a build or launch. Before real-world use, independently check current site rules, jurisdictional requirements, applicable model-rocketry safety codes, motor certification, and appropriate supervision. The documents are references and do not replace those checks.
